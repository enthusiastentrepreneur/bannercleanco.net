Why Lisdoonvarna Is a Different Kind of Cleaning Job

Lisdoonvarna has always drawn visitors — first for the sulphur spa, now for the Burren, the walking and cycling trails, and the Matchmaking Festival that turns the town into one of the busiest spots in Ireland for a few weeks each autumn. That means a lot of local housing stock does double duty as a holiday let or short-term rental, and a lot of it is older, stone-built cottages that need a proper hand rather than a rushed once-over. When the town fills up, changeover windows shrink to a couple of hours between one guest leaving and the next arriving — there's no room for a cleaner who's running late from a job in Ennis or Limerick.

Also Covering the Villages Around Lisdoonvarna

We're based in Lisdoonvarna, but most of our week is spent across North and Mid Clare:

  • Doolin — ten minutes down the road, and the ferry terminal for the Aran Islands and gateway to the Cliffs of Moher. Self-catering cottages and B&Bs here turn over constantly in season, so we work around your ferry times, not the other way round.
  • Lahinch — the surf town. Holiday homes and short lets get booked back-to-back through summer, and a Saturday changeover often collides with your own family plans.
